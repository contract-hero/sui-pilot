1. What happened

The two skills were removed from the plugin at the user's request. As part of the cleanup, 27 lines matching ⤳ skill: move-code-review or ⤳ skill: move-code-quality were deleted from agents/sui-pilot-agent.md.

The user chose whole-line deletion over the alternative of keeping each warning as a plain → note. That decision was made with the trade-off stated in advance. This register is the record of what that cost.

The nature of the loss

Those lines were not pure routing. Most carried a parenthetical that asserted a security or idiom claim. The claim existed only on that line — it is not restated elsewhere in the graph, and it is not in the bundled doc corpora in that compressed form.

│   ⤳ skill: move-code-review (never use timestamps, tx hash, or coin balances as randomness)
    ╰──────────┬──────────╯ ╰────────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────╯
          routing target                        the part that was worth keeping

Of the 27 deleted lines, 20 carried a substantive claim and 7 were routing only.

Two lines were structural, not textual. One deleted line was a tree node header (├── Address-owned) whose pointer sat on the same line; deleting it orphaned three child detail lines. Three others were └── subtree terminators. All four were repaired during the cleanup — the Address-owned node was restored and Gas model / Kiosk were re-parented. No action needed, recorded here so a future diff reader is not confused by those repairs.

6. Open questions for later

No decision has been made on recovery. These are the options as they stand, not a recommendation.

Where should the 20 claims live?

  • Back in the agent graph as plain notes. Re-add each claim at its original anchor as a → line with no skill pointer. Cheapest option, restores the always-loaded reach. Costs ~20 lines against the 1200-line ceiling — the file currently sits at 941, so there is room.
  • A new consolidated skill. Fold them into a single security-notes skill invoked on demand. Keeps the graph slim, but the claims stop being always-loaded, which is what made them useful.
  • Absorb into the surviving skills. The arithmetic claim (line 625) fits oz-math; several authorization claims could fit specify. Partial coverage only — most have no natural home.
  • Leave them out. Accept the loss. Valid if the judgement is that the bundled corpora cover the same ground well enough in longer form.

Unresolved from the cleanup

.claude-plugin/plugin.json still describes the plugin as offering "code quality tools". That phrase no longer maps to a specific skill. It was left untouched because the string is marketplace-facing.

Do not re-add the pointers verbatim. Any recovery must drop the ⤳ skill: move-code-review / ⤳ skill: move-code-quality prefix. Those skills no longer exist, and a pointer to a missing skill is worse than no pointer.
